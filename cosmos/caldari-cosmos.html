Caldari COSMOS

Caldari COSMOS is a collection of PvE content located in Okkelen constellation in The Forge region and in 09-4XW constellation in Tenal region. This includes COSMOS mission agents, COSMOS combat sites and unique rats on belts.

High security Caldari COSMOS

The high security COSMOS is located in Okkelen (The Forge).

Agents 0 / 0

Do not even talk to the agent unless you are going to run the mission. Failing the mission or letting it expire will prevent you from ever doing that mission or any mission in the same chain. As soon as you talk to the agent you get a mission offer that expires in seven days.

COSMOS missions demand high enough faction standing to gain the missions — corp standing cannot be used instead. They also require higher standing than normal missions: 2.00 for L2, 4.00 for L3 and 6.00 for L4 (normally 1.00 / 3.00 / 5.00). Checking an agent here marks the site they are located at and the shopping-list items required for their missions. Agents tagged Repeatable offer the repeatable Improving Relations mission.
